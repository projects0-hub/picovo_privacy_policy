Changes and contact Privacy Policy Picovo – Photo Gallery · Android package: com.picovo.gallery Last updated: November 24, 2026 Picovo is a gallery app designed to help you browse, organize, edit and protect your photos and videos on your device. Local media processing is separate from the analytics, diagnostics, consent and advertising information that integrated services may transmit over the internet. 1. About this policy This policy describes how Picovo (the “app”, “we” or “us”) handles information when you use its Android application. Picovo does not require you to create an account or sign in using an external account. Contact us at rjslabs.support@gmail.com with privacy questions. 2. Local media and permissions With your permission, Picovo accesses photos and videos on your device to display galleries and albums, search, edit, play, organize, mark favorites and manage deleted media. These gallery operations are performed locally. Picovo does not upload your gallery photos, videos, file names, album names, file paths, search queries or extracted image text to our servers as part of these features. Depending on your Android version and the feature used, Picovo may request access to photos and videos, camera access, biometric authentication and notifications. Camera access is used for camera-related features you choose to use. Network access supports the online services described below. You can manage permissions through Android settings; some features may stop working if you deny their required permissions. If you deliberately share media or send an attachment through another application, the receiving application or recipient handles that information under its own practices. 3. Private Vault, Trash and storage Private Vault media is encrypted on the device using Android Keystore-backed encryption. PIN or pattern verification information is stored in encrypted preferences. Biometric verification uses Android authentication mechanisms; Picovo does not receive or upload your biometric templates. Trash, favorites, edits and search indexes are managed locally. The app disables Android automatic app backup and includes exclusions for vault files and encrypted preferences. Keep your own safe backups of important media; uninstalling, clearing app storage or losing access to encryption keys may result in loss of vault data. 4. Information collected by online services Depending on your device, region, consent choices and enabled services, Picovo and its integrated SDKs may transmit: • Approximate location inferred from IP address, such as country or region; Picovo does not request precise GPS location permissions. • Device or installation identifiers, including Android Advertising ID (when available), App Set ID, Firebase Installation ID, Analytics app-instance ID, and SDK-specific identifiers. • App interactions, session information, screen and feature usage, and advertising impressions, clicks and other ad interactions. • Crash reports, ANR reports, diagnostics, device and operating system information, network information and performance measurements. • App version, language or locale, consent-related information and configuration request information. These categories do not mean that your gallery media is uploaded. Certain identifiers and operational telemetry may be collected automatically even if you decline personalized advertising. 5. Firebase services Picovo uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand app usage, feature interactions and sessions; Crashlytics and Firebase Sessions to diagnose crashes, ANRs and stability problems; and Remote Config and Firebase Installations to retrieve app configuration and manage installation-specific service operation. Analytics events use feature names, screen/source tags and grouped counts rather than file names, album names, paths or search text. Firebase services may process identifiers, approximate geography, device information, usage events and technical telemetry. Some Firebase components initialize at app startup. Analytics and crash reporting are distinct from personalized ad consent. Learn more: Firebase privacy and security · Google Privacy Policy. 6. Google ML Kit Picovo uses Google ML Kit for on-device image labeling and text recognition to support media search. Image pixels, recognized text and resulting local indexes remain on your device for these features. ML Kit may transmit technical diagnostics, model download information, latency measurements, device information and installation identifiers for service operation and improvement. Learn more: ML Kit terms and privacy information. 7. Advertising and mediation Picovo may display advertising through Unity LevelPlay, including ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. Advertising availability and placements may vary by app version, remote configuration, device and region. These services may collect and share advertising identifiers, App Set ID, IP-derived approximate location, device and network information, diagnostics, ad requests, impressions, clicks and other ad interactions for advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Android advertising and attribution services may also be used by these SDKs. Picovo uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and present privacy or consent information where applicable. UMP is a consent service, not an indication that Picovo currently serves ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Some SDK startup components may run before the consent interface appears; active LevelPlay initialization and ad loading are governed by the app's consent flow. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily stop all advertising, identifiers or operational data processing. Learn more: Unity Privacy Policy · ironSource Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy. 8. Regional privacy checks To determine applicable regional privacy options, Picovo may use device region signals and HTTPS requests to IP-geolocation services operated by country.is, Cloudflare and IPinfo. These providers necessarily receive your public IP address and ordinary connection information when responding to a request. Picovo uses the resulting country or region information to select relevant privacy and consent handling, not to obtain precise GPS coordinates. Provider websites: country.is · Cloudflare Privacy Policy · IPinfo Privacy Policy. 9. How information is used and shared Information described above is used to operate app and SDK features, retrieve configuration, measure usage, diagnose technical issues, deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, protect security and support applicable privacy requirements. Firebase and ML Kit process relevant telemetry in connection with their services. Advertising partners may receive approximate location, identifiers, app and ad interactions and diagnostic information for their advertising and operational purposes. Regional lookup providers receive connection information as described above. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or when necessary to protect rights or security. User-initiated sharing and external applications are handled separately. 10. Your privacy choices • Manage media, camera and other app permissions in Android settings. • Where offered, manage advertising privacy options through Picovo's About screen and Ad privacy settings, including relevant regional consent or opt-out controls. • Use Android privacy settings to reset or delete your advertising ID where supported, and manage advertising personalization options. • Use the privacy rights channels provided by the relevant third-party services, including Unity's privacy resources. Available choices depend on your device, region and app version. Advertising choices may not disable analytics, crash reporting, consent checks or all other SDK data processing. 11. Retention and deletion Local media and app settings remain on your device until removed through relevant app or Android controls. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove app-private data and local identifiers, but does not necessarily erase media in shared storage or information already transmitted to third-party services. SDK information is retained according to the applicable provider policies, service configurations and operational or legal requirements. We do not promise that all SDK information is deleted within a specific period. Picovo has no user accounts or comprehensive request mechanism that can identify and delete all SDK telemetry by email; installation and advertising identifiers may not be linked to your email address. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence we hold, subject to applicable legal requirements. You may also contact relevant SDK providers to exercise available rights concerning information they process. 12. Data security Picovo uses HTTPS/TLS for its direct online requests and the integrated services described above use encrypted transport. Private Vault uses on-device encryption. These safeguards reduce risk but no software, storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. 13. Feedback and support If you contact us or use Picovo's feedback or problem-reporting feature, your chosen email application may send your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond and investigate your request. Please avoid including sensitive personal information or private media unless necessary. Contact rjslabs.support@gmail.com for support or correspondence deletion requests. 14. Children's privacy Picovo does not offer child accounts or require users to provide their age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us. Applicable age and consent requirements should be followed when using the app and its online services. 15. Regional privacy rights Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or consent withdrawal, and certain advertising-related uses of personal information. Contact us with a request and we will assess it under applicable law and our ability to identify the information. For information independently processed by third-party providers, their privacy policies explain available request channels. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority. 16. Changes and contact We may update this policy as Picovo's features, SDKs and privacy practices change. The revised policy will be made available at this page with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact the Picovo developer at rjslabs.support@gmail.com.
